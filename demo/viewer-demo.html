Standalone sld-substation-viewer

The viewer used on its own — no editor, no plugin host, no shell. Each substation in the document is rendered by its own sld-substation-viewer. With interaction left in its default locked state, the viewer is a truly static read-only diagram: no connect ports, resize handles or context menus are drawn, nothing is pointer-interactive, and no interaction intents are dispatched. An editor opts back in by driving the viewer to idle and the gesture modes.
